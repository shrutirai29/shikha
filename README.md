## 🚀 Overview

Knottiingale is a full-stack e-commerce web platform engineered for artisanal crochet creations, custom gifts, soft toys, and home decor. The application includes:

- **Customer Storefront:** Fast browsing, interactive 3D yarn stage preview, product filtering, responsive cart, transparent pricing with GST calculation, and multi-address management.
- **Payment & Checkout:** Integrated with **Razorpay** (UPI, Cards, Net Banking) and **Cash on Delivery (COD)**.
- **Compliance & Legal:** Complete customer policy pages (Terms & Conditions, Privacy Policy, Shipping & Delivery Policy, Cancellation & Refund Policy, Contact & Custom Orders).
- **Admin Management Panel:** Comprehensive catalog control, category management, order processing & tracking status updates, customer review moderation, and revenue analytics.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Frontend:** React 19, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Lucide Icons, Framer Motion, Three.js / React Three Fiber.
- **Backend:** Node.js, Express, TypeScript, MongoDB (Mongoose), JWT Auth, Razorpay SDK, Nodemailer / Brevo HTTPS email API.
